# About

Welcome to my humble abode. Don't be shy, take a seat. Since you have clicked on the 'About' text, I assume you want to know more about me and the _content_ I put on this website. If not, I won't be offended if you close the tab. A bit odd, but to each their own.

I aim to write tutorial and showcase posts about a diverse range of computational topics, mainly bioinformatics, machine learning, and statistics. The word that comes to my mind is _recreational informatics_, since I like to play with ideas and identify new connections between concepts; and learn, always learn new things. To a large extent, I write just for myself (but you are welcome to join in!).

How to describe myself? I am curious and I like to explore topics that I find difficult. And I have too many interests. I started this blog to force myself to write and polish projects, and I am happy to report it has worked really well. There is something to be said about the benefits of the "shut up and do it" mentality. Sometimes it's about not overthinking what you know is a mechanically simple action. Sometimes it's about not listening to the part of your brain that says "wait, think about it a little bit more". Sometimes it's about letting yourself believe that something is good enough.

In that sense, I think I am the worst kind of artist: talented enough to see the flaws but not good enough to correct them. Well. Here is me shutting up and doing it. It's messy and dirty, and it's easy to get lost in it. But also, it is primordial, complex, and provides a unique space that houses a rich ecosystem. Welcome to the bog. Be careful not to step on the frogs.

Mikel
